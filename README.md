# Orespawn Test

A simple hobby project — my attempt at remaking an old mod from my childhood.

## Showcase only

This repo exists purely to showcase what I've been working on. I do **not** own any of the assets in here (textures, sounds, models, etc.) — they were copied from the original mod, which belongs to its original creators.

Because of that, please don't treat this as something to download, build, or redistribute. Nothing in here is mine to distribute; it's just here so people can look at it.

## About

A Minecraft Forge mod that re-adds some of the ores, blocks, tools, and food items I remember from the original mod, written as a learning exercise.
